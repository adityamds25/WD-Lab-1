Tribute to A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

About Vikram Sarabhai

Dr. Vikram Sarabhai (1919–1971) was a pioneering Indian physicist and astronomer recognized as the "Father of the Indian Space Program". He founded the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL) and established the Indian Space Research Organization (ISRO), driving space technology for development. Awarded the Padma Bhushan (1966) and Padma Vibhushan (1972, posthumous), he was instrumental in establishing IIM Ahmedabad and the Thumba rocket station.

Achievements

  • India's First Satellite (Aryabhata): He initiated the project for the fabrication and launch of an Indian satellite, which resulted in Aryabhata being put into orbit in 1975.
  • First Rocket Launch Station: Along with Homi Bhabha, he established the Thumba Equatorial Rocket Launching Station (TERLS) in 1963, launching India's first sounding rocket on November 21, 1963.
  • SITE Project: He negotiated with NASA in 1966 for the Satellite Instructional Television Experiment (SITE), which brought television education to remote Indian villages.
  • Founder of ISRO: He was the principal force behind the establishment of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) in 1969.
